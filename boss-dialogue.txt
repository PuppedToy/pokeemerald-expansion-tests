========================================================================

HOW TO USE THIS FILE
  • This is a GENERATED file (scripts/extract-boss-dialogue.mjs). Edit the dialogue text
    freely; when you are done, tell me and I will fold your edits back into the game
    scripts. Re-running the extractor OVERWRITES this file, so edit a copy if unsure.
  • Each line of dialogue is tagged  [label @ file:line]  — do NOT change those tags;
    they are how your edited text is mapped back to the exact .string in the ROM source.
  • Buckets per boss: PRE-BATTLE / IN-BATTLE INTRO / ON-DEFEAT / POST-BATTLE, plus an
    "OTHER TEXT IN THIS MAP" net (same character, may or may not belong to the scene).
  • Rival bosses have 6 trainer variants (May/Brendan × 3 starters) that usually share
    the same text — identical variants are collapsed.
